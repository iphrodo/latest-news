## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Category tabs panel
The system SHALL show a tab panel with the following news categories: Artificial Intelligence, Technology, Finance, Gadgets, Digital Currencies, Playstation, Apple, IT Jobs, Transfers & Rumours, as well as a tab with the European football news feed (covering the top domestic leagues and major UEFA club competitions).

#### Scenario: Displaying tabs
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the page
- **THEN** the system shows a panel with all category tabs, and one of them is marked as active
